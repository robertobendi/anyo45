01

Hillside Residence

Batangas · Residential

A stepped family home that follows the contour of the slope. Pitched roofs throw deep shade across the upper terrace, timber louver screens filter the hard western sun, and sage-tinted glazing opens the living level to the valley. The massing breaks the volume down so a large house still reads as a cluster of smaller, climate-tuned forms.

Drawn from concept through to the construction-document set a contractor can price and build.
