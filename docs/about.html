The practice

About

A small Batangas architectural studio with a simple discipline: design homes that belong to their climate, and draw them precisely enough to build.

The name

Anyo & Disenyo

In Tagalog, anyo means form and disenyo means design. Together they describe how we work: every house starts with form — how a family lives, gathers, and rests — and is resolved through design that answers where it stands.

A climate-first practice

Batangas is hot, bright, and wet, with sea air never far away. We design for it directly: orientation that ducks the western sun, deep eaves and louvered screens for shade, cross-ventilation that keeps rooms cool without machinery, and honest materials — stone, timber, concrete — that age well in the tropics.

The result is tropical-modern: clean, grounded houses that feel calm and current, and stay comfortable in a Philippine climate rather than fighting it.

Drawn to be built

We are a licensed architectural practice, and we work like one. Projects move through clear, numbered stages and end in a complete construction-document set — drawings detailed enough that a contractor can price and build them with confidence. Licensure and credentials are available on request.

Where we work

Rooted in Batangas

We are based in Batangas City and work across the province and nearby Calabarzon. Being local means we know the sites, the sun, the trades, and the way people here want to live — and we can be on site when it matters.

Off Facebook and on our own ground, this is the studio's home base — a place to see the work, understand the process, and start a conversation.
