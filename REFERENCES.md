# REFERENCES

## Overview
The three user references are the firm's **own** brand assets — not third-party website screenshots — so they set *direction* through identity and imagery rather than through a finished layout. **ref-01** (the SketchUp-style tropical-modern render) is the **primary** reference: it best captures what this firm sells and what the audience is buying, and its earthy, naturalistic, render-led aesthetic should dominate. **ref-02** (the ghosted/transparent concept render in greenery) is **secondary**, informing a more conceptual/atmospheric imagery treatment and reinforcing the olive-and-glass-green palette. **ref-00** is the **identity anchor** — the locked logo lock-up and brand color, not a layout cue. Net direction: a clean, architectural, earth-toned site that lets render imagery carry the page. No reference dictates a specific grid or motion language, so layout is open within a disciplined, professional register.

## ref-00.png

### Typography
Wordmark only. Personality: clean **humanist/geometric sans**, all-caps, with generous tracking on the sub-line. "ANYO at DISENYO" reads close to **Gill Sans / Questrial / Tenor Sans** territory (note the lowercase, smaller "at" as a connective). Sub-line "ARCHITECTURAL SERVICES" is lighter weight and wider-tracked. Closest Google Fonts analogs: **Tenor Sans** or **Questrial** for the airy geometric feel; **Archivo** / **Inter** if a more neutral workhorse is wanted. Weight contrast is mild (one medium weight, one light); case is UPPERCASE with a single lowercase connective; tracking normal-to-expanded.

### Palette
`#6E6C3E — muted olive / moss — logo mark + wordmark accent (signature)`
`#5C5C5C — graphite gray — primary wordmark`
`#8C8C8C — warm mid-gray — sub-line / secondary text`
`#F2F3F0 — pale cool off-white — surface / background`

### Layout signature
Centered, symmetrical lock-up: a wide horizontal monogram (interlocking "a"+"d" chain-link / figure-eight) stacked over two centered text lines, set on a near-white field with comfortable margin. The recognizable move: **the rounded, looping monogram reading as a continuous unbroken ribbon** — calm, balanced, architectural.

### Imagery treatment
None — pure identity on a flat off-white ground.

### Decoration & texture
Minimal. The only graphic element is the monogram itself; no badges, patterns, grain, or ornament. Clean print-identity minimalism.

### Motion / texture cues
Static, still, composed. Implies restrained, professional motion only (quiet fades, no flash or bounce).

### One-line mood
"A licensed architect's calling card in moss green."

## ref-01.png

### Typography
Only the small olive-outline logo watermark appears (top-left), so type direction is inferential. The render's discipline and calm argue for a **clean architectural sans** for the site — humanist sans body (Inter / Manrope) with the geometric wordmark feel of **Tenor Sans / Questrial** for headings; optionally a quiet **transitional serif** (Source Serif 4 / Newsreader) for project narrative if a touch of warmth/editorial credibility is wanted. No display drama; weight contrast should stay moderate.

### Palette
`#D7D6CB — warm gray-green haze — sky / background`
`#4A4A46 — charcoal slate — pitched roofs`
`#B9925A — warm teak — timber louvers / screens`
`#ECEBE4 — soft off-white — building walls`
`#7E9A86 — muted sage glass — tinted glazing`
`#6E6C3E — olive — logo watermark (brand tie-in)`

### Layout signature
A wide, horizontal architectural render bleeding edge to edge, viewed from a raised three-quarter angle so the building recedes diagonally across the frame. Foreground roof masses anchor the lower-left; the brand watermark sits quietly top-left. The signature move: **a full-bleed environmental render with the subject set on a strong diagonal**, letting the architecture itself be the hero — a model for hero/section imagery on the site.

### Imagery treatment
SketchUp/CAD-style **architectural visualization** with a soft, lightly hand-drawn line quality and matte, slightly desaturated grading. Naturalistic warm-neutral palette, low-to-medium contrast, hazy sky. Wide environmental crop (whole building in context). Untouched/no frame — render presented straight.

### Decoration & texture
Subtle sketch-line texture inherent to the render; faint paper-like softness in the sky. No added stickers, borders, or ornament.

### Motion / texture cues
Print-still and contemplative. Suggests slow, gentle reveal/parallax on scroll at most — nothing snappy or playful.

### One-line mood
"Tropical-modern home, drawn in a quiet afternoon haze."

## ref-02.png

### Typography
Again only the gray logo watermark + "ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN" sub-line (bottom-center). Inferred direction matches ref-01: restrained architectural sans, with room for a light serif accent for concept/narrative text. The thin, wide-tracked sub-line reinforces an airy, spaced uppercase treatment for eyebrows/labels.

### Palette
`#F4F6F3 — near-white vignette — sky / negative space`
`#8FA98A — sage / eucalyptus green — tinted glass + lawn`
`#3E3E3C — charcoal — structural frame / dark masses`
`#7C8F5E — olive foliage — bamboo / planting`
`#A9B6A2 — pale celadon — ghosted glass planes`
`#6E6E6E — gray — logo watermark`

### Layout signature
Square composition: a translucent, **X-ray/ghosted** building floats among trees and bamboo, dissolving into a feathered white vignette at the edges, with the centered logo lock-up sitting in the clean lower whitespace. The signature move: **architecture rendered semi-transparent so structure and landscape read through each other** — an atmospheric, conceptual treatment usable for an "approach/philosophy" section or a layered hero.

### Imagery treatment
Conceptual architectural **visualization with transparency/X-ray** — green-tinted glass volumes, ghosted planes, watercolor-soft feathered edges. Cool-warm-neutral grading dominated by greens; airy, high-key, low-contrast. Set in lush planting (documentary-of-an-idea rather than a built photo). Soft-vignette treatment; no hard frame.

### Decoration & texture
The feathered white vignette and the translucency are the "texture." Foliage (trees, bamboo) acts as organic framing. No graphic ornament, badges, or patterns.

### Motion / texture cues
Still, ethereal, drifting. If translated to motion: slow fades and layered opacity transitions; never abrupt.

### One-line mood
"A glass house imagined in a bamboo grove."
