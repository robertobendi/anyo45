# ANALYSIS

## Business
Anyo at Disenyo is an architectural firm based in Batangas City, Philippines, offering architectural design and related services (the source descriptor reads "an architectural firm with service expertise on…" before truncating — most likely residential and small-commercial design, construction documentation, and design supervision). The name is Tagalog — *anyo* (form/shape) + *disenyo* (design) — i.e. "Form and Design," a deliberate statement of the firm's craft. Its work, shown in its own renders, is tropical-modern Filipino architecture: white planes, dark pitched roofs, timber louvers, glass, and lush planting. Revenue comes from professional design fees (concept-through-construction-documents, likely with construction supervision or design-build add-ons). The only public presence found is a Facebook page, so the firm currently has no real website.

## Audience
- **Primary**: Filipino homeowners and families in/around Batangas planning to build or renovate a custom house — typically 30s–55s, middle to upper-middle income, who have land or are buying it and want a designed home rather than a generic contractor build. In the moment they are sizing up whether this firm is credible, sees their vision, and is worth a consultation call/message.
- **Secondary**: small developers and lot owners commissioning multi-unit or commercial projects; contractors and engineers as collaborators; prospective hires (junior architects/draftspersons); and referral sources (past clients sharing the page).

## What they're trying to convey
- "Real, qualified architects — not just a draftsman or a contractor's free plan." (the deliberate "Architectural Services / Architectural Design" sub-line)
- "We do considered, modern, livable tropical homes" — the renders signal taste and climate-appropriate design, not catalog house plans.
- "Established and trustworthy" — a settled monogram identity and a Tagalog name signal a local, rooted practice; unintentionally, the Facebook-only footprint reads as small/early-stage.

## What's broken about the current presentation
- **No website at all** — the entire presence is a Facebook page behind a login/cookie wall; anonymous visitors see almost nothing of the brand.
- **No discoverability or control** — Facebook frames the brand in its own blue chrome; the firm owns no narrative, no SEO, no contactable home base.
- **Work is undersold** — the firm has genuinely attractive renders, but they sit buried in a feed rather than presented as a portfolio with project context.
- **No clear conversion path** — there is no obvious "request a consultation," services list, scope, or process; a serious prospect has nothing to act on.
- **Low social proof on the surface** — ~281 likes [verify] is the only visible signal; no testimonials, credentials (PRC license / professional affiliations), or completed-project count.

## Primary objective
Convert a prospective homeowner/developer into a consultation inquiry (call or message). Secondary: present the portfolio credibly enough to establish the firm as a real, licensed architectural practice; and give the firm an ownable, shareable home base off Facebook.

## Voice
**Grounded, precise, warm.** Writes like a working architect talking to a homeowner — plain, confident, climate- and lifestyle-aware, never salesy or jargon-heavy. Leads with the client's home and life, backs it with quiet professional credibility (licensed, experienced), and uses the firm's Tagalog name as a small point of pride rather than a gimmick. Bilingual touches (Tagalog/English) are natural for the Batangas audience but should stay light and clear.

## Constraints the next stage must respect
- **Olive/moss green (`#6E6C3E`) is the locked brand color** — it ties the logo to the firm's own renders (planting, tinted glass, timber). It must anchor the palette; pair it with warm timber, charcoal, sage, and a clean off-white.
- **The monogram lock-up is fixed brand equity** — geometric, rounded, restrained. The aesthetic direction should feel architectural and disciplined; this brand does not support maximalist/decorative or Y2K/grunge families.
- **Imagery will be architectural renders** (the firm's SketchUp/concept visualizations), not stock or flash photography — directions must be built to showcase render-style project imagery, and should be robust to soft, slightly-low-contrast source images.
- **Sole user instruction is "make a professional website for this firm"** — read as: clean, credible, conversion-oriented; no instruction locks typography or layout, so type/layout direction is open within a professional, architectural register.
- **Bilingual, Philippines-local context** — copy and any localized labels should suit a Batangas/Filipino audience; name meaning (*form & design*) is an asset to carry forward.
