## Candidate A — Soft modern × Slow editorial luxury

**One-line signature**: A single full-bleed tropical-modern render fills the viewport on a near-white field, with the firm name set in 5.5rem light Fraunces serif resting low-left over deep negative space and one hairline olive rule — the render breathes, the type whispers, nothing else competes.

**Palette**:
- `#F4F6F3` — eucalyptus mist — surface / background
- `#FBFBF9` — gallery white — raised cards / second surface
- `#2E2E2B` — charred slate — ink
- `#6E6C3E` — moss olive — accent (signature, locked)
- `#8FA98A` — eucalyptus sage — secondary accent / tint blocks
- `#B9925A` — warm teak — highlight / underline
- `#8C8C8C` — drafting gray — captions / eyebrows

**Typography**:
- Display: Fraunces at weight 340 (oversized, optical display cut)
- Body: Hanken Grotesk at weight 400
- Accent / eyebrow: Tenor Sans at weight 400 (UPPERCASE, expanded tracking — lifted from the wordmark's own geometric-humanist feel)

**Hero composition**: Image-led, type-quiet. One ref-01-style environmental render bleeds edge to edge with the building on its raised three-quarter diagonal; a soft top-down white scrim lets the headline sit legibly in the lower-left third. Display line in light Fraunces ("Form and design for the way you live in the tropics"), a Tenor Sans eyebrow above it ("ARCHITECTURAL SERVICES · BATANGAS"), one olive hairline rule, and a single text "Request a consultation" link. No badges, no stickers — just architecture, air, and a slow fade-in on scroll.

**Decorative kit**:
- Hairline olive rule — under every eyebrow and section title
- Figure captions — small drafting-gray "Fig. 01 — Residence, Batangas" beneath each render
- Embossed monogram watermark — faint tonal mark, lower corner of hero and About, echoing the render watermarks
- Single teak underline — on the active nav item and primary link only
- Generous baseline whitespace — full-viewport breathing gaps between sections as the primary "ornament"

**Voice angle**: Single-statement editorial lines with deep breathing room — one confident sentence per spread, the Tagalog name returning as a quiet refrain ("*anyo* — form; *disenyo* — design").

**Brand**: Keep the monogram lock-up exactly as observed, in `#6E6C3E` olive on the eucalyptus-mist field; reuse the thin tonal outline version as the on-render watermark. No redraw — only spacing cleanup for screen use.

**Why this fits**: Most faithful application of the references — ref-01 and ref-02 both let the render carry the frame inside soft, high-key negative space, and this candidate makes that the entire layout logic. It reads instantly as a licensed, tasteful practice (the ANALYSIS primary objective: credibility → consultation), and the restrained register suits homeowners sizing up whether the firm "sees their vision" without any salesy noise.

**Palette**:
- `#ECE7D9` — drafting cream — paper surface (subtle tooth)
- `#27271F` — bistre black — ink / type
- `#6E6C3E` — moss olive — primary block / brand (locked, dominant field)
- `#B9925A` — teak ochre — accent block 2
- `#7E9A86` — celadon sage — accent block 3 (from ref-01 glazing)
- `#C24A33` — muted brick — single spark accent (numerals, primary button)

**Typography**:
- Display: Archivo Expanded at weight 700 (caps, for headings + oversized section numerals)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans at weight 400 (architectural / drafting workhorse)
- Accent / eyebrow: IBM Plex Mono at weight 500 (UPPERCASE labels, coordinates, dates, spec rows)

**Hero composition**: Type-and-block led, render framed not bled. Left two-thirds: a flat moss-olive block holding the firm name in heavy Archivo Expanded caps with a Plex Mono kicker ("EST. BATANGAS · LIC. ARCHITECTS"). Right third: a single render cropped hard into a precise rectangle with a teak-ochre keyline. A bold bistre baseline rule runs full width beneath; a brick-red "REQUEST CONSULTATION →" button anchors the lower-left. The looping monogram appears once, large and rotated, ghosted into the olive block as a tonal pattern.

**Decorative kit**:
- Oversized section numerals — `01`, `02`, `03` in Archivo Expanded opening every section
- Color-block panels — alternating olive / teak / sage full-bleed bands structuring the page
- Monogram-as-pattern — the figure-eight mark tiled or rotated as a quiet tonal divider
- Mono spec rows — Plex Mono key/value lines ("SCOPE — Residential design + CD") under each project
- Thick baseline rules — heavy bistre hairline closing each section, Swiss-grid style

**Voice angle**: Confident, structured, spec-sheet clarity — declarative headlines, numbered process steps, labeled fields; credibility communicated through order and precision.

**Brand**: Keep the monogram, but promote it to a working graphic system — a single-color olive mark in the header, redrawn at one consistent stroke weight, and reused rotated/tiled as a tonal motif. Wordmark rebuilt in Archivo Expanded caps so it locks to the new display family while the figure-eight stays untouched.

**Why this fits**: The monogram is already a Rand-grade geometric mark (a continuous looping ribbon), so a Swiss/mid-century system is the native extension of the firm's own identity (ref-00) rather than an imposed style. It reads as established, organized, and licensed — directly answering the ANALYSIS gap of "looks small/early-stage" — and the spec-row + numbered-process structure gives a serious homeowner or small developer concrete things to act on (the conversion objective).

This candidate turns the firm's renders into a portfolio with project context and voice — the journal format showcases ref-01's storytelling and ref-02's atmospheric X-ray treatment side by side — while the editorial restraint (no grunge, geometric monogram intact) keeps it credible and on-brand for an upper-middle homeowner audience.

## Self-check
The three are visibly distinct in a single screenshot: A is airy photo-led minimalism (one render, deep whitespace, light serif), B is a flat Swiss color-block grid (heavy expanded caps, mono specs, brick spark), and C is a dense layered journal spread (drop caps, marginalia, mixed serif+sans, collaged frames). Only A is restrained; B and C both commit hard to personality, and none use the forbidden maximalist/Y2K/grunge families — appropriate for a disciplined architectural brand. All three anchor on locked moss olive `#6E6C3E`, carry the brand's teak/sage/charcoal/off-white as functional accents, build around render-style imagery, and preserve the figure-eight monogram. Font kits are fully separated across candidates (Fraunces/Hanken/Tenor · Archivo Expanded/Plex Sans/Plex Mono · Newsreader/Familjen/Space Grotesk) and avoid the Inter+Lora / Manrope+Source Serif defaults.
